The importance of the airway
Airway is at the top of the primary survey because if compromised it has the greatest capacity to worsen PT morbitiy and mortality (perhaps second only to expsoure to danger, or unresolved catastrophic haemorhage) 
Airway should be the first thing to be assessed on every patient, and the promptly reassessed whenever there is an acute deterioration in PT stability, particularly alertness.

How to fix an airway
An airway is either occluded or patent. And these occlusions can be positional or due to foreign body.
The management for forgein body should priortise the removal of it, reaching for suction, laryngoscopes, forceps, but not forgetting that appropriate positioning will aid its removal.
The management for positional occlusions should follow a graded approach, utilising the least invasive (and convinently the least time-consuming) method that maintains airway patentcy.
Remeber that fixing the airway at one time, or from one occlusions, does not mean it will remain patent. Remain vigilant for reocclusion, and reassess whenever the patient is moved.
