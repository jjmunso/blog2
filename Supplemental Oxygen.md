Oxygen is a common intevention provided to patients in the prehospital space. It's really important to understand its role as a therapy and to have a specific framework for utilising it in the management of patients.

Who needs oxygen?
As a genearl rule, patients with hypoxemia <94 require oxygen. Some patients will benefit from empiric oxygen, and some patients who need oxygen will not have an accurate reflection of that demand in the spO2. Exmaples indlue patients who are in a state of shock, have cold extremities, are anemic.
